Street Lessons

The game should teach what matters when it matters, not dump a static checklist on you.

Just in time
Legitimate Ventures — Cash & Fronts

Legitimate fronts for laundering. Capital expands them, but they generate zero return without assigned crew to handle operations.

The Other Ledger — Underground Operations

High-yield operations run in backrooms. Highly profitable, but every expansion level and assigned crew member builds police **Heat**.

Street Operations — Muscle & Respect

Muscle generates **Respect** passively at the cost of drawing **Heat**. Cop bribes and informants keep the precinct off your back.
